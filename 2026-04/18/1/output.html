Classic Pour Over Recipe

Ingredients:
- 25g freshly ground coffee
- 400g filtered water (just off boil)

Instructions:
1. Place filter in dripper and rinse with hot water.
2. Add ground coffee and gently shake to level.
3. Start timer. Pour 50g water to bloom for 30 seconds.
4. Slowly pour remaining water in circles until 400g total.
5. Let drain completely (about 2:30-3:00 minutes).
6. Remove dripper, swirl, and enjoy.

Tip: Adjust grind size or pour speed for your preferred strength and flavor.

How to Make a Matcha Latte at Home

WHAT IS MATCHA?
Matcha is more than just a trend. It’s a centuries-old practice rooted in mindfulness, flavor, and focus. Made from finely ground green tea leaves, our matcha offers calm, sustained energy and a clean, vegetal sweetness that’s deeply satisfying.

We’ll walk you through what makes matcha special and how to make a dreamy, vibrant matcha latte at home. No café needed—just a whisk, warm water, and a few quiet minutes.

A Matcha Recipe You’ll Keep Coming Back To

Balanced, smooth, and deeply calming!
  • 1 teaspoon (2 g) Matcha - Organic & Ceremonial-Grade
  • 2 ounces hot water (175°F / 80°C recommended)
  • 6-8 ounces milk of your choice, hot or iced (oat, almond, soy, or dairy all work well)

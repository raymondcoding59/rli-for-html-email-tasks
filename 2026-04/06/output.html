Sencha, Sakamoto Koshu
From the Sakamoto family garden in Shizuoka, Japan, this sencha is more than a cup of tea. It’s a daily ritual in mindfulness. Grown organically and shaded before harvest, Koshu is rich in umami and packed with antioxidants, making it a perfect addition to your wellness routine.
Whether you’re cutting caffeine, seeking clarity, or simply slowing down, this green tea offers a clean, restorative experience that is earthy, floral, and beautifully grounding.
ORDER SENCHA, SAKAMOTO KOSHUN
 

HOW TO BREW

Tea Recipe
Hot: Measure 4g of tea for every 400ml of water.
Cold: Measure 5g of tea for every 200ml of water.
Heat water to 181 degrees.
Steep for 5 minutes.

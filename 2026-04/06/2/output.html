Tea Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon tea leaves or 1 tea bag
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • Milk (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Boil the water in a saucepan.
  2. Add tea leaves or tea bag and let it steep for 3-5 minutes.
  3. Add sugar and milk if desired. Stir well.
  4. Strain into a cup and enjoy your tea!

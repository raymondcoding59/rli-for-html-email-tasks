Learn how to make a creamy matcha latte at home.

Matcha is more than just a trend. It’s a centuries-old practice rooted in mindfulness, flavor, and focus.

We’ll walk you through what makes matcha special and how to make a creamy, vibrant matcha latte at home.

HAVE YOUR MATCHA MOMENT
A Matcha Recipe You’ll Keep Coming Back To
Balanced, smooth, and deeply calming:
  • 1 tsp ceremonial matcha
  • 2 oz hot water
  • 6–8 oz milk of choice
Sift matcha into a bowl and whisk with hot water until smooth. Pour into mug and add milk.

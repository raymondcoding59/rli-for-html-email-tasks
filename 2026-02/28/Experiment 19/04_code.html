A Matcha Recipe You’ll Keep Coming Back To
Balanced, smooth, and deeply calming:
  • 1 tsp Organic Matcha
  • 2 oz hot water
  • 6–8 oz milk of choice
Sift matcha into a bowl and whisk with hot water until smooth and frothy.
